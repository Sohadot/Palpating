# Source Register — v0.1

## S-001 — Robotic tissue palpation
**Title:** A Minimally Invasive Robotic Tissue Palpation Device  
**Year:** 2024  
**URL:** https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11178256/  
**Use:** Supports the factual premise that robotic palpation systems can quantify soft-tissue stiffness.  
**Does not support:** General diagnosis, longevity outcomes, or the Palpating category thesis itself.

## S-002 — Wearable mechanical sensing
**Title:** Wearable mechanical and electrochemical sensors for real-time health monitoring  
**Year:** 2024  
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s43246-024-00658-2  
**Use:** Supports the premise that mechanical signals can be captured by wearable sensing mechanisms and converted into electrical signals.  
**Does not support:** A unified “physical signs” standard.

## S-003 — Soft sensing and haptics
**Title:** Soft sensing and haptics for medical procedures  
**Year:** 2024  
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41578-024-00653-6  
**Use:** Supports the premise that tactile information and haptic feedback are active engineering concerns in medical procedures.  
**Does not support:** The term Digital Palpability.

## S-004 — Multimodal wearable sensing
**Title:** Hybrid multimodal wearable sensors for comprehensive health monitoring  
**Year:** 2024  
**URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-024-01247-4  
**Use:** Supports the broader trajectory toward multimodal wearable sensing.  
**Does not support:** Claims that more modalities automatically produce better clinical outcomes.

## S-005 — Mechanical-property imaging
**Title:** Magnetic resonance elastography in a nutshell: Tomographic imaging of soft tissue viscoelasticity for detecting and staging disease with a focus on inflammation  
**Year:** 2024  
**URL:** https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39645347/  
**Use:** Supports the premise that mechanical properties of tissue can be represented as quantitative parameter maps.  
**Does not support:** Equating elastography with palpation or Digital Palpability.
