# Buyer Logic — Palpating

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Binding · Subordinate to `ASSET_THESIS.md`.
Implements factory layer 11 and the methodology's
`BUYER_LOGIC_AND_VALUE_MAXIMIZATION.md`.

> A sovereign asset is not sold. It is **acquired**. Selling negotiates value down;
> acquisition recognises value up.

Every construction decision is evaluated against its effect on the acquisition
argument. The question is never "will a buyer like palpating.com?" It is: **"what
becomes unavailable to them if they do not acquire it?"**

---

## 1. Strategic buyer classes

Palpating is built to be attractive to several classes at once, so no single
buyer's absence collapses the position:

- **Wearable / digital-health platforms** moving beyond vital-sign dashboards.
- **Medical robotics** companies.
- **Tactile / haptic sensing** companies.
- **Digital examination** platforms.
- **Medical imaging / elastography** players.
- **Health-AI** platforms.
- **Longevity** companies wanting to move past vital-sign dashboards.
- **Sensor manufacturers.**
- **Institutional entrants** into physical-computational health.

### Top three and the problem each has

| Rank | Buyer | Existing problem Palpating answers |
|------|-------|-------------------------------------|
| 1 | Wearable / digital-health platform beyond vital signs | Their vital-sign axis is commodity. They need an owned, credible frame for the next axis — and would rather own the adopted one than cite a competitor's. |
| 2 | Medical robotics / tactile-haptic sensing | They build physical-property sensing but have no governed language, standard, or map to position it as "physical-sign intelligence." |
| 3 | Imaging / elastography / digital examination | They quantify mechanical tissue properties but have no published frame linking them to a broader physical-sign category and its evidence map. |

## 2. Buyer typology (how each values the asset)

- **Strategic acquirer** (in/adjacent to the domain): buys *position*. Values
  category-artifact status, imitation barrier, institutional interface, thesis
  credibility. Discounts current revenue. → Position it as a **capability
  acquisition**, priced from replacement cost.
- **Institutional entrant** (entering the domain): buys *time and credibility*.
  Values reference-layer depth and an adoptable thesis. → Emphasise the years of
  position-building the acquisition compresses.
- **Content-portfolio acquirer**: buys *cash flow*. Lowest multiple; not the
  target buyer. We do not optimise for them.

## 3. The Loss-in-Not-Acquiring answer

Maximal when: Palpating is the **only** governed reference system for
physical-sign intelligence; its vocabulary (**Digital Palpability**, **Physical
Sign Map**) is adopted by others; its thesis is the frame practitioners use; and
its quality is visibly higher than a buyer could produce quickly.

What cannot be rebuilt fast — the loss the buyer inherits by not acquiring:
the **name**, the **language**, the **ontology and standard**, the **property ×
modality × evidence dataset**, the **mapper**, the **accumulated reference and
link graph**, the **governance record**, the **agent-legibility footprint**, and
the word **Palpating.com** above all of it (`ASSET_INTELLIGENCE_FACTORY_PLAN.md`
§9). Owning a competitor with that position becomes a strategic threat — which is
exactly the effect a correctly built asset produces.

## 4. Value-maximization rules (binding on construction)

1. Never initiate from a **revenue-dependent** position — argue position, not
   multiples.
2. **Build before revealing** — the asset's value at conversation must far exceed
   its value when first acknowledged available. (Directly implies the acquisition
   section must come *off* the live site now — `ACQUISITION_READINESS.md`.)
3. **Qualified interest only** — a buyer who cannot preserve the quality standard
   damages future value.
4. **Price from replacement cost**, not revenue multiple.
5. **The asset must not need the acquisition to succeed** — it is designed to be
   viable as a permanently held asset; disposition is a decision from strength.

## 5. When to consider disposition

Only when the six maximal-value signals hold (thesis referenced as a frame;
vocabulary adopted where the asset is not cited; no equivalent asset at the same
quality; interface visibly ahead; reference layer with gatekeeping authority;
inbound interest without outreach). Earlier trades leverage for speed; later
risks erosion. Tracking is governed by `MEASUREMENT_AND_ARCHIVAL.md` and
`ACQUISITION_READINESS.md`.

> **Build the asset a strategic buyer would have to build if they did not acquire
> it — then let the position pull.**
