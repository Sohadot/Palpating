## 1. The Governing Thesis

> Digital health made **vital signs** computationally legible: heart rate,
> respiration, temperature, pressure, oxygenation — a small set of scalar
> physiological values that fit a dashboard. Palpating extends that same logic
> to **physical signs**: the mechanical, structural and tactile states of the
> body — stiffness, elasticity, deformation, pressure, mobility, texture — that
> can increasingly be sensed, represented, transmitted, compared and
> interpreted computationally.

This is a **position**, not a description. It is specific enough to be wrong.
A domain expert could contradict it — by arguing that physical signs are not a
coherent computational category, or that "vital signs" already subsumes them.
The asset exists to defend the position that they are a distinct, emerging,
governable category: **Physical-Sign Intelligence**.

### The category the asset owns

**Physical-Sign Intelligence** — the governed understanding of which physical
properties of the body can be made *digitally palpable*, by which sensing
modalities, within which limits, and on what evidence.

Not "palpation education." Not "digital health" in general. Not a medical
service. A governed interpretive frame for the transition from vital signs to
physical signs.

### The two owned sentences

- **From vital signs to physical signs.** — the category framing.
- **Making the physical state of the body computationally legible.** — the
  interpretive sentence.

### The central term

**Digital Palpability** — the extent to which a physical property of the body
can move from a sensed or physically-interacted-with state into a digital
representation that can be measured, transmitted, compared or interpreted.

Digital Palpability is the asset's owned vocabulary. It is a *conceptual
framing*, never a clinical score, diagnostic standard, or validated medical
metric. See `CATEGORY_LANGUAGE.md`.

## 3. Reclassification of the Current Site

The site currently live at `palpating.com` is hereby reclassified as:

> **Palpating v0.1 — Thesis Demonstrator.**

It succeeded at the hardest and most valuable step: it discovered and stated the
thesis ("From vital signs to physical signs" / "Making the physical state of the
body computationally legible"). It is kept and used as a **discovery artifact**.

It is explicitly **not** the sovereign asset. Measured against the Category
Artifact Standard and the Intelligence Factory Doctrine, it still lacks almost
all of the real moat:

- No governed **Classification Architecture** — the physical-sign landscape is a
  concept list, not an ontology with entry criteria.
- No **Standard**, no reproducible **Protocol**, no **Engine** producing
  intelligence.
- Sources exist (`SOURCE_REGISTER.md`) but the **Reference Layer** is not yet a
  catalog governed by admission criteria.
- No structured intelligence corpus, data layer, or public archival/versioning
  architecture.
- No measured category adoption.
- The interface preceded a governed design system; the correct order is
  Thesis → Classification → Design System → Foundation → Method → Reference.

Therefore v0.1 will **not** be patched cosmetically as if colour or the hero
were the problem. It is a demonstrator we build the correct system *over*.

### One immediate governance correction it requires

The current interface carries an **Acquisition** section ("Acquire the
identity…", "Acquisition inquiry") and an Acquisition nav item. This is
marketplace / acquisition-urgency framing on an asset surface, which violates
portfolio **Non-Negotiable #10 (No desperation)** and the Buyer Logic rule

replaced with a discreet Owner / Contact reference only. Recorded as a required
