# Acquisition Readiness — Palpating

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Binding · Subordinate to `ASSET_THESIS.md` and
`BUYER_LOGIC.md`. Governs *when and how* the asset may signal availability, and
enforces **Build before revealing**.

---

## 1. Posture: build in silence

Non-Negotiable #10 forbids desperation: no acquisition-urgency language, public
buyer targeting, or marketplace framing on any asset surface. Buyer Logic Rule 2
(**Build before revealing**) makes this a construction rule, not just a tone rule:
an asset visibly being built *in order to be sold* signals desperation and moves
pricing down.

Therefore, until the maximal-value signals hold, the public surface carries **no**
acquisition framing — only a discreet Owner / Contact reference. A serious buyer
(e.g. one who has publicly advertised intent) can be addressed directly, off the
asset, using the domain itself. The site never shouts "for sale" while authority
is still being built.

## 3. Readiness signals (all six required before disposition talk)

From `BUYER_LOGIC_AND_VALUE_MAXIMIZATION.md`, tracked in
`MEASUREMENT_AND_ARCHIVAL.md`:

1. The governing thesis is used as a **frame** by others in the domain.
2. The owned vocabulary appears in discussions where Palpating is **not** cited.
3. **No equivalent** governed asset exists at the same structural quality.
4. The interface visibly **outperforms** the closest comparable by a redesign-sized
   margin.
5. The reference layer has enough depth that its **entry criteria are tested** by
   parties who want to be listed.
6. **Inbound** interest arrives without outreach — the position pulls.

Initiating before all six trades leverage for speed. Initiating after risks
competitive erosion. Neither is done casually.

## 4. If an unsolicited approach arrives before readiness

- Do not enter valuation from a revenue-dependent position.
- Qualify the buyer against the institutional register (Rule 3) before any
  numbers.
- Frame from **position and replacement cost**, never a revenue multiple.
- Record the approach and the decision in `DECISION_LOG.md`. Do not alter the
  public surface to court it.

> **The asset must not need the acquisition to succeed. Build first; reveal only
> from strength.**
