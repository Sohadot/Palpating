METHODOLOGY / v0.1

Make the framing
stronger than the claim.

Palpating is built around a narrow proposition: parts of the body's physical state can increasingly be sensed, represented and interpreted computationally. Everything public on this site must remain inside that boundary.

Core thesis

From vital signs to physical signs.

The phrase is a category framing, not a declaration that “physical signs” are a newly discovered medical class. It highlights a shift in attention from familiar scalar measurements toward a broader set of mechanical, structural and tactile properties.

Digital Palpability

Digital Palpability is Palpating.com's conceptual term for the extent to which a physical state can be sensed, represented, transmitted, compared or interpreted computationally.

It is deliberately not expressed as a score, ladder, standard or diagnostic metric. No numerical validity is implied.

Claims boundary

  • No diagnosis or treatment advice.
  • No claim that a specific sensor or modality can infer health status beyond what its cited evidence supports.
  • No claim that physical-sign sensing extends lifespan or healthspan.
  • No implication that “Digital Palpability” is an established clinical term.
  • No invented benchmarks, rankings, scores or certifications.

Evidence discipline

Established facts are tied to external sources. Palpating's own framing is labeled as conceptual language. Where evidence is indirect, the site uses terms such as “can,” “may,” “emerging,” or “conceptual” rather than converting technical possibility into clinical certainty.

Versioning

This is version 0.1 of the public thesis. Future revisions should change only when the evidence base, terminology or strategic positioning materially changes. The repository decision log records such changes.
