# Claims Boundary

Palpating.com is a conceptual and reference asset, not a medical service.

## Allowed
- Describe established research on tactile sensing, tissue stiffness, mechanical sensing, haptics, wearables, robotics, and elastography when tied to sources.
- State that selected physical properties can be measured or computationally represented where supported by evidence.
- Introduce “Digital Palpability” explicitly as Palpating.com's conceptual language.
- Explore strategic implications for digital health, sensing, robotics, remote care and physical-state intelligence as forward-looking analysis.

## Prohibited without new evidence and governance review
- Diagnostic or treatment recommendations.
- Disease detection claims not made by a directly cited source.
- Claims that sensing physical signs improves longevity, lifespan or healthspan.
- Numerical Digital Palpability scores, certifications, rankings or maturity levels.
- Presenting Palpating terminology as an accepted medical standard.
- Implying endorsement by cited authors or institutions.
