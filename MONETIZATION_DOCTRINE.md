# Monetization Doctrine — Palpating

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Binding · Subordinate to `ASSET_THESIS.md`.
Implements factory layer 10 and the methodology's
`operating-rules/publication-and-monetization-gates.md`.

**Revenue is a consequence of authority, never a substitute for it**
(`SOVEREIGN_ASSET_EXECUTION_ORDER.md`). It is not a design constraint that
precedes quality. No income layer activates before structural integrity is
validated (Gate 5) and the monetization gate passes.

---

## 1. The governing rule

> **The factory may sell depth. It may never sell conclusions.**

The free Digital Palpability Mapper returns a **real, complete map**. Paid layers
extend analysis; they never unlock a verdict withheld from the free tier. Any
revenue a strategic buyer would have to **remove on acquisition** is not built
(`BUYER_LOGIC.md`).

## 2. What Palpating must never do

Forbidden unconditionally (Non-Negotiable #5; `INTERFACE_GOVERNANCE.md`):
advertising, sidebar/affiliate banners, sponsored-link injection, "book a
consultation" on every page, urgency widgets, pop-up lead capture interrupting
doctrine, or any pattern that converts the Mapper into a lead-capture trap.
Revenue that lowers trust is forbidden; revenue that raises trust is permitted,
disclosed, and reviewed.

## 3. The revenue ladder (phase-ordered)

| Phase | Output | Buyer | Why it raises trust |
|-------|--------|-------|---------------------|
| **Free authority** | Digital Palpability Mapper (basic) | Anyone | Builds adoption, backlinks, and language diffusion; the map is honest and complete. |
| **Paid depth** | Digital Palpability Brief | MedTech / wearable / robotics teams | A deeper property × modality × evidence landscape — a reference artifact the buyer keeps, not a gated answer. |
| **B2B** | Physical-Sign Coverage Audit | A specific company | "Which physical states can your system actually represent, and what remains outside digital legibility?" Reference-grade; positions Palpating as the standard, not a vendor. |
| **Intelligence** | Physical-Sign Technology Landscape | Investors / strategy | Governed vendor/research intelligence over the ontology. |
| **Later** | Structured data / API access | Platforms, agents | Property ↔ modality ↔ representation ↔ evidence, machine-readable. |
| **Later still** | Taxonomy / standard / reference-data licensing | Platforms, standards bodies | Licensing the frame — pure category ownership; only with real adoption. |

Each output is a **governed surface**: named, versioned, described on a public
reference page, traceable to the protocol/ontology version that produced it, and
carrying no claim forbidden on a public page (Output Doctrine,
`INTELLIGENCE_FACTORY_DOCTRINE.md`). Free and paid outputs may never contradict
each other.

## 4. The claims firewall applies to revenue

No paid output may make a claim `CLAIMS_GOVERNANCE.md` forbids. A Coverage Audit
reports **representation coverage**, never a client's clinical fitness. A Brief
extends evidence and mapping, never a diagnosis. Selling depth about a
*representation architecture* is permitted; selling a *health conclusion* is not.

## 5. The gate

Before activating any income layer, run the five publication-and-monetization
gates; **Revenue Compatibility** must Pass: monetization must strengthen, not
weaken, authority. If it weakens authority, it is not activated — regardless of
the revenue on offer.

> **Authority first. Revenue follows. If the money would cost trust, the money is
> not built.**
