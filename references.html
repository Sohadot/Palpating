REFERENCE LAYER / v0.1

Evidence before
extrapolation.

Palpating's category thesis is intentionally forward-looking. Its factual substrate is anchored only to what the cited literature supports: measurable mechanical properties, tactile sensing, haptic feedback and multimodal health sensing.

Source policy

Sources are selected for direct relevance to the conceptual bridge between bodily physical state and computation. The presence of a source here does not mean the authors endorse the Palpating framing or the term “Digital Palpability.”

No source is used to support a claim that Palpating.com provides diagnosis, treatment or validated longevity outcomes.
